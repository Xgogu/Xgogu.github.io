I am currently a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at Zhejiang University. Prior to this, I received my Bachelor's degree in Robotics Engineering from Chongqing University, where I was a member of the Honor Class at the National School of Exceptional Engineers.

My research interests lie in Embodied AI, Multimodal Large Language Models (VLM/VLA), and the development of high-quality synthetic data pipelines for robotics. I focus on enhancing robots' spatial understanding and building a complete technical chain from data generation to embodied manipulation.
